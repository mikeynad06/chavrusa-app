# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 6, 2026

ChavrusaApp ("we," "our," or "us") runs a platform that helps people find study partners ("chavrusas") for Torah learning. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who can see it, and the choices you have.

By using ChavrusaApp, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

**Account information you give us:**
- Name
- Email address
- Password (stored only as a secure one-way hash; we cannot see or recover your password)
- Location and timezone
- WhatsApp number (optional). If you add one, it is stored on your profile and is **not shown to other users**.

**Information you provide while using the app:**
- Study requests you post (topic, level, learning style, description, timing and location preferences)
- Topic and location alerts you set up on your profile
- Messages you send to a matched study partner in the in-app chat

**If you sign in with Google:**
- We receive your name and email address from Google. We do not receive or store your Google password.

**Information created by the service:**
- Whether your email address has been verified, and short-lived verification and password-reset codes (stored only in hashed form).
- Basic technical information, such as IP address and browser type, which our hosting providers record in ordinary server logs. We use it for security, preventing abuse, and troubleshooting.

**On your device:** we store a login token in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. We do not currently use advertising cookies or third-party analytics trackers. If that changes, we will update this policy.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Create and run your account, and confirm your email address
- Show your study requests to other users
- Connect you with a study partner when a request is claimed
- Send you emails: account emails (email verification, password reset), a notice when someone claims your request, and alerts when a new request matches the topics and locations you chose
- Let you and a matched study partner message each other in the app
- Keep the platform secure, prevent abuse and fix problems
- Respond to messages you send us

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

## 3. What Other Users Can See

- **Your study requests** are visible to other logged-in users. This includes the details you choose to put in the request, so don't include anything you want to keep private.
- **When a request is claimed,** the other person sees your name and can message you in the in-app chat.
- **Your email address, password and WhatsApp number are not shown to other users.**

## 4. Messages and What You Choose to Share in Chat

Messages in the in-app chat are visible to you and to your matched study partner, and we store them so the conversation is available to both of you.

**Anything you type in the chat is shared with the other person by your choice.** This includes contact details such as a phone number, WhatsApp number, email address, home address or social media account, and any other personal information. Once you share something, we cannot control what the other person does with it, whether they save it, repeat it, or contact you outside the app.

Please be careful about what you reveal. We recommend that you:
- Do not share financial information, passwords, identification numbers or other sensitive details.
- Consider getting to know your study partner in the chat before sharing personal contact details.
- Stop the conversation and contact us if anyone makes you uncomfortable.

**We are not responsible for information that users choose to share with each other, whether in the chat or elsewhere, or for how other users use it.** More detail is in our Terms of Service.

We do not routinely read private messages. We may access them if needed to investigate abuse or a safety concern, to enforce our Terms, or to comply with the law.

## 5. Who We Share Information With

We use service providers to run ChavrusaApp. They process information only to provide their service to us:
- **Vercel:** hosts the website
- **Railway:** runs our server
- **Supabase:** stores our database, including accounts, requests and messages
- **Resend:** delivers our emails
- **Google:** provides Google sign-in, if you choose it

These providers may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States and parts of Asia.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety and rights of our users or the public.

We do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

## 6. How Long We Keep Information

We keep your account information for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account (see section 7), we permanently delete it straight away, except for anything the law requires us to keep.

Copies may remain in our database provider's backups until those backups expire, and our email provider keeps its own delivery logs of emails we have sent you. Because messages are shared with the other person in a conversation, a copy they have saved or received is outside our control.

## 7. Your Choices

- You can update your name, WhatsApp number and topic and location alerts on your Profile page at any time.
- You can stop alert emails by removing your topic and location alerts on your Profile page. We will still send account emails, such as email verification, password resets and notice that your request was claimed.
- You can cancel a request you posted at any time.
- You can delete your account at any time from your Profile page, as described below. If you cannot log in, contact us at the address in section 11 and we will help.

### Deleting your account

- **It is permanent and immediate.** There is no undo and no grace period.
- **How you confirm.** If your account has a password, you enter it and type DELETE. If you signed up with Google, you type DELETE and then confirm through a link we email you, which expires after 30 minutes.
- **What is deleted:** your profile (name, email address, WhatsApp number, location and timezone), your topic and location alert settings, and every request you posted.
- **Conversations are deleted for both people.** Every match you are part of, and all of its messages, including the messages the other person wrote, is deleted. The other person loses that conversation too.
- **Requests you had claimed become open again,** so their owners can find someone else. We email those people to say the conversation has ended, without naming you.
- **We email you a confirmation** once your account is deleted.
- **Backups and logs.** Copies may remain in our database provider's backups until they expire, and our email provider keeps its own delivery logs.
- **Counts.** Anonymous totals that do not identify you, such as the number of pairs made, may change when matches are deleted.

## 8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. Passwords and one-time codes are stored as one-way hashes, connections to the site are encrypted (HTTPS), and sign-in attempts are rate-limited. No method of storing or sending data is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Keep your password private.

## 9. Children's Privacy

ChavrusaApp is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and change the "Last updated" date above.
